01 · Mission

We build cognition from first principles.

Most AI systems are a single model. EloAI is eight systems — each a distinct cognitive faculty: intention, perception, memory, wonder, emotion, reasoning, connection, and reflection. They share a substrate and pass structured data between them.

They are not prompt engineering. They are math. Two are shipped and validated today — Perception (the ELO substrate) and Memory (Mneme) — and the rest are built in sequence on top.

Our work is documented in our research thesis and published as findings appear in our field notes.

02 · Philosophy

Long bets, published honestly.

Research moves fast when you publish only wins. We don't do that. Our field notes include negative results, surprises, and things we got wrong — because that's what real research looks like.

Each system is a long-horizon bet on a specific cognitive faculty. We build them in sequence on a shared substrate, and a result in one frequently reshapes the design of the next.

03 · Values

How we operate.

01

Independence first

No external investors dictating research direction. The programs we run are the ones we believe are genuinely important — not the ones that are easiest to pitch.

02

Measured, not claimed

We publish numbers we've actually measured — 20.1% fewer tokens, sub-2ms recall, byte-exact round-trips. If a result is on the site, it has been validated.

03

Honest publishing

Findings go up as they happen, positive or negative. The field notes are unfiltered — including the experiments that taught us what doesn't work.

04 · Infrastructure

One substrate. Every system.

Everything runs on ELO — a canonical vocabulary of 374,189 entries where every word and phrase maps to an integer ID. The substrate is the contract; every system above it speaks ELO.

Memory is Mneme: temporal-wave storage with 4D cosine retrieval, no ML dependency, activation under 2ms across 10,000 seeds. One vocabulary, one memory, shared by all eight systems.

The runtime is Irin — the orchestrator that walks each Seed through the pipeline. Before Irin runs, the Logic Matrix scores the Seed across twelve activation dimensions and rejects roughly 70%. Attention as suppression, not amplification.
